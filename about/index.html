A free book swap in Barcelona

Bookswappin’ BCN is a free shelf for our WhatsApp book community in Barcelona. Add the books you’ve finished, and find your next read.

How it works

  1. Make an account. Pick a username and a 6-number PIN, then add your name and the part of Barcelona you live in.
  2. Add your books. Type the title or scan the barcode. The cover is found for you. Choose if you want to swap, lend or give it away.
  3. Find your next read. Search by title, author, person or area, and filter by language and genre.
  4. Ask the owner. Tap “Ask in the WhatsApp group” on any book and a message is ready to send.
  5. Meet up and swap. Agree on a place in Barcelona, then the owner marks the book as reserved, lent, swapped or given away.

Three ways to share a book

  • Swap: you give a book and get one back.
  • Lend: you want it back once it’s read.
  • Give away: it’s theirs to keep.

Who it’s for

Anyone in Barcelona and nearby towns who reads and wants to pass books on instead of letting them gather dust. There are books in English, Spanish, Catalan and other languages, and you can choose to only see the languages you read.

Free, with no ads

Bookswappin’ BCN is run by people from the group, for the group. There’s nothing to pay and no ads. The site also has a wishlist, so you hear when a book you want shows up, and badges for the books you share.
