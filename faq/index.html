Questions about Bookswappin’ BCN

Quick answers about swapping books in Barcelona with our community.

What is Bookswappin’ BCN?

A free website where people in Barcelona swap, lend and give away books they’ve finished. It belongs to a WhatsApp book community, where members ask for books and arrange meetups.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and nothing to pay. It’s run by people from the group, for the group.

How do I swap a book in Barcelona?

Make an account, add the books you’re done with, and browse the shelf. When you find one you like, tap “Ask in the WhatsApp group”, agree on a place in Barcelona and meet up.

Do I need to be in the WhatsApp group?

Yes. That’s where you ask for books and agree on a meetup. You can join it from the site.

Which languages are the books in?

English, Spanish, Catalan and others, depending on what members bring. You can filter the shelf to only show the languages you read.

What’s the difference between swap, lend and give away?

Swap means you give a book and get one back. Lend means you want it back once it’s read. Give away means it’s theirs to keep.

Which areas does it cover?

All of Barcelona, from Gràcia to Sants, plus nearby towns. Each book shows the area its owner lives in, so you can find books close to you.

What if a book I want isn’t on the shelf?

Add it to your “Looking for” list. When someone adds it in one of your languages, you get a notification on the site.

Can I add it to my phone’s home screen?

Yes, and it then opens like an app. On iPhone use Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android use Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app.

Who can see my profile?

Anyone with the link can see your name, username, area, picture and books. You can delete your account at any time from your profile.
